## Engine Interpretation

- `P0_BLOCK`: title alone is enough to block new buys in backtest and live overlay.
- `P1_HIGH`: high-risk warning; title can trigger risk reduction/watchlist, PDF/LLM optional for explanation.
- `P2_REVIEW`: candidate risk/complex event; keep signal, and use PDF/LLM when position impact is material.
- `P3_POSITIVE_CANDIDATE`: record only in phase 1; no positive alpha boost until event-study validation.
- `P4_NEUTRAL`: routine archive/discard for warning engine.
